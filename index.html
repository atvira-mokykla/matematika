How it works

  1. Your year, like at school. Autumn and spring semesters, units in the order of the national model plan, about four lessons a week, a test at the end of each semester.
  2. One lesson at a time. Each lesson starts with a question, explains with figures you can move, and ends with a short check that marks your answers at once.
  3. See how far you are. Lessons tick themselves when you finish the check, and you can tick lessons you did in class. Set a weekly goal; progress bars show the unit, the semester and the year.

Parents: pick your child's grade to see what the class learns this year and when. Progress is saved only on the device your child uses; nothing is sent anywhere.

Teachers: each year page is a ready long-term plan with programme codes, and every lesson has a projector view, teacher notes and printable worksheets with answer keys.

Your progress stays with you

There are no accounts and no tracking. What you have seen and answered is saved only in this browser, on this device. Anyone using the same browser can see it, and clearing the browser's data deletes it. You can move it to another device with a file.
